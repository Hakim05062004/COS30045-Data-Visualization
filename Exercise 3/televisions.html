Efficiency Optimization Guidelines

  • Ambient Light Sensor: Enable ambient lighting adjustment to automatically dim backlights in dark rooms.
  • HDR Adjustments: High Dynamic Range significantly increases brightness and power consumption.
  • Power Down: Turn off media consoles and smart TV set-top boxes at the wall switch when inactive.
