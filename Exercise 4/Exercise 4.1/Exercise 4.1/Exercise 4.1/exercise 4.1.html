Step 2: SVG Coordinate System Mapping

SVG coordinates position the origin (0, 0) at the top-left corner. Horizontal progression spans rightward ($+x$), while vertical progression extends downward ($+y$).

  • Canvas Boundaries: viewBox="0 0 600 500" sets total coordinate dimensions to 600 units wide by 500 units high.
  • Apex Coordinate: The roof peak is positioned at (300, 140).
  • Wall Boundaries: Defined by <rect x="180" y="240" width="240" height="140">, spanning horizontally from 180 to 420 and vertically from 240 to 380.
  • Group Translation: The windows share an identical coordinate design inside a <g> element, translated to (205, 270) and (350, 270) without duplicating shape coordinates.
